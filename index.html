Privacy Policy for Privo

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Privo respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use the Privo mobile application.

Information We Collect

Privo may collect the following information:

  • Email address used for registration and authentication
  • Approximate location used to find relevant chat partners
  • User-generated content, including messages and photos sent in chats
  • Device and technical information needed for security and app functionality
  • Reports, blocks, and safety-related information

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Connect users for anonymous conversations
  • Provide messaging and disappearing photo features
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, spam, and inappropriate behavior
  • Improve application performance and security
  • Respond to support requests

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the application, including hosting, database, authentication, notification, and analytics providers.

We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect users, prevent fraud, or enforce our terms.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user data. Data is transmitted using encrypted connections. However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security.

Messages and Photos

Messages and photos may be temporarily stored to provide chat functionality. Disappearing photos are intended to become unavailable after the configured viewing period. Users should not send sensitive, illegal, or confidential content.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to operate the application, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain safety.

Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting us at the email address below.

Children's Privacy

Privo is intended only for users who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
