Privo Child Safety Standards

Privo has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Prohibited Content and Behavior

The following content and behavior are strictly prohibited in Privo:

  • Child sexual abuse material or any sexual content involving minors.
  • Sexual exploitation, grooming, or solicitation of minors.
  • Requests for sexual images or videos involving minors.
  • Sharing, creating, uploading, or distributing child sexual abuse material.
  • Sexualized, manipulated, or AI-generated images of minors.
  • Blackmail, threats, or coercion involving intimate content.
  • Attempts to arrange sexual contact with minors.

Reporting Violations

Users can report accounts, messages, and inappropriate behavior directly inside the Privo application using the Report feature available in chats and user profiles.

Enforcement

Privo may remove prohibited content, suspend or permanently ban accounts, preserve relevant information, and report suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation to appropriate law enforcement authorities.

Age Requirement

Privo is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older.
